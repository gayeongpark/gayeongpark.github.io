# First journal : My Pre-Trip Preparations for Volunteering in Uganda

The year 2025 is very meaningful to me because it’s the year I can finally showcase what I’ve studied so far. To explain, after returning to Korea from Germany, it was difficult for me to find a job related to computer science. During that time, I was deeply focused on studying security and earned several certifications in network and Active Directory pentesting. It seemed like that field suited me well. However, even though I was happy with the positive results from my studies, I wanted to find a way to apply and share what I had learned.

By chance, I was able to get a position as an after-school computer and coding instructor at an elementary school, as well as a visiting instructor for elementary and middle schools. At first, I felt a bit sad because I hadn’t gotten a job in the field I originally wanted. But as I started teaching kids what I had studied, I found great fulfillment in those moments, and I really enjoyed the work. For example, when I prepared lessons carefully and created fun programs with the kids, seeing their faces light up with excitement and curiosity was so adorable. Their reactions motivated me to study even more and provide them with great lessons. I became fully immersed in teaching and have been happily working at the school since March.

Then, in July, I received the exciting news that I had been accepted for a KOICA volunteer position in Uganda, which I had applied for on a whim. Teaching students has been a valuable experience, but I wanted to broaden the scope of whom I could teach and gain more diverse international experience. The main goal in Uganda is to train local computer instructors and help improve the work efficiency of regional government officials. Next week, I expect to spend a busy time at the KOICA Global Talent Training Center, planning activities with my team.

I feel very sorry for the students I have been teaching so dedicatedly until now. It’s sudden that I have to leave, and I feel bad about it. But I chose this path because I believe that after gaining more experience, I can come back and offer even better lessons to the students. Today, I wrote this blog post to share my recent updates and why I decided to apply.

In my next post, I plan to write about a day at the KOICA Global Talent Training Center.

Happy teaching😊
